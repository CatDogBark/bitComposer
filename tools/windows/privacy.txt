bitComposer: privacy

bitComposer composes and plays music on your computer. It collects nothing
about you, your music or how you use it, and sends none of it anywhere.

One thing goes over the network on its own: when the player starts, it asks
GitHub's public releases list (api.github.com) whether a newer version exists,
so it can offer the UPDATE button. That request carries only what any web
request does (your IP address and the program's name). Turn it off in the
player under SET > PLAYER > UPDATES.

Updating downloads only when you press UPDATE (the installer, from GitHub).

Uninstall from Settings > Apps > Installed apps, or the Start menu's
"Uninstall bitComposer". Your music and settings stay where they are.

Full privacy and code signing policy:
https://github.com/CatDogBark/bitComposer#privacy
