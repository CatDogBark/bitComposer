THE WRITER2.0 · what GENERATE writes

What GENERATE writes in 2.0. Each song is drawn whole from a vocabulary of counted habits: how the band is balanced, how its figures repeat and vary, where the tune comes back. The shipped vocabulary was learned from the readings of Final Fantasy VI's 67 songs, as statistics with none of the game's notes or samples, and these songs play on bitComposer's own sound set. They are seeds 1 to 5 as the writer gives them. Nobody picked them, and a seed writes the same song on every machine.

    CLASSIC1.x · still in 2.0

    The 1.x generator, still in 2.0: WRITER: CLASSIC under SET in the player, --classic on the command line. One song for each of its six sounds at a fixed seed: four SNES style packs and the Genesis and chip palettes. They're written again when Classic's sound changes; these six were written by 1.0.0 and still play note for note the same.
